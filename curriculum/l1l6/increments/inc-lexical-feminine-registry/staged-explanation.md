# Staged explanation (learner-facing)

**Stage 1** — most feminine Arabic nouns wear a mark (ة/اء/ى), but a
handful are feminine by pure convention with NO mark at all: أرض "earth",
شمس "sun", ريح "wind", نار "fire", حرب "war".

**Stage 2** — these words just have to be memorized — a machine (or a
learner) can't derive "feminine" from their shape, so this pack only ever
recognizes the exact words on its list.

**Stage 3** — a masculine word (قمر "moon") gets no vote from shape either
way, and a regularly-marked feminine (مدرسة) is a totally different,
shape-based mechanism this registry doesn't touch.

**Stage 4** — a word that merely LOOKS related (أرضية "flooring", built on
أرض's root) is not the registered lexeme itself — only the exact word
counts.
