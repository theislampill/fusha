# Staged explanation (learner-facing)

**Stage 1** — a preposition is not one English word in disguise. The same
Arabic preposition can mark where something started, what it is made of, part
of a larger thing, or which aspect is meant.

**Stage 2** — the sentence chooses, not the preposition. Look at the verb
first: a verb of asking picks a different sense than a verb of moving, even
with the same words after it.

**Stage 3** — then check the complement, and then run the paraphrase. If
"made of" keeps the meaning, you have the material sense. If you have not run
the test, you do not have the sense yet, and our card says so.

**Stage 4** — one thing never changes: the noun after these prepositions is
genitive in every sense. So the ending tells you nothing about which sense
won, and the sense tells you nothing you did not already know about the
ending.
