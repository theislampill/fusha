# Staged explanation (learner-facing)

**Stage 1** — مَا is several words wearing one spelling: "not", "which/that
which", "what?", a verb-to-noun wrapper, "whatever", and two grammatical
uses.

**Stage 2** — the sentence, not the word, tells you which: ما قَرَأَ =
"he did not read" (verb after it), أَنْفِقُوا مِمَّا... = "from that which"
(relative), ما اسمك؟ = "what?" (question).

**Stage 3** — sometimes two readings genuinely fit. Real scholarship keeps
both and says so — and so do we, on the page.

**Stage 4** — so never memorize مَا = "what". Memorize the QUESTIONS you ask
the sentence.
