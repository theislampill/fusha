# Staged explanation (learner-facing)

**Stage 1** — a plain nominal sentence has both halves in raf': الطالبُ
مجتهدٌ.

**Stage 2** — كان seizes the second half: كان الطالبُ مجتهدًا. إنّ seizes
the first: إنّ الطالبَ مجتهدٌ. Opposite grips, easy to swap — that swap is
the most common learner error in this topic.

**Stage 3** — ظنّ and its sisters grip BOTH: ظننتُ الطالبَ مجتهدًا.

**Stage 4** — watch for impostors: لكنْ without shadda just means "but" and
grips nothing.
