# Staged explanation (learner-facing)

**Stage 1** — a word starting with a mim can be a doer, a done-to, an action
name, a place, a time, or a tool. The consonants will not tell you which.

**Stage 2** — the vowels will. The vowel on the mim itself splits tool from
place, and the vowel before the last root letter splits doer from done-to.

**Stage 3** — two of these shapes are genuinely identical, so even a fully
vowelled word can carry two readings, and only the sentence around it decides.

**Stage 4** — and when the text is unvowelled, the evidence you need simply is
not on the page. Say so. Guessing the commoner class is how a wrong gloss gets
into a dictionary.
