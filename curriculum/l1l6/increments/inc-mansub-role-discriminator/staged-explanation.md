# Staged explanation (learner-facing)

**Stage 1** — the accusative ending on a bare noun after a verb is a doorway,
not an answer. Four different jobs come through that same door.

**Stage 2** — ask the noun a question. Does it say HOW the action was done, or
WHY, or in what CONDITION someone was, or in what RESPECT the statement is
true? Each question belongs to a different job.

**Stage 3** — also check the root. If the noun is built from the verb's own
root, reinforcement is in play; if it is a different root, motive comes into
view.

**Stage 4** — sometimes the root says one thing and the question says another.
That is not a failure of the tests; it is a genuinely open sentence, and the
honest answer keeps both.
