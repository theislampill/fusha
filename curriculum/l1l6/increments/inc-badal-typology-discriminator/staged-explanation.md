# Staged explanation (learner-facing)

**Stage 1** — sometimes a second noun does not describe the first: it stands
in for it, carrying the same case and the same job in the sentence.

**Stage 2** — four flavours. The same thing named again. A piece of it. A
quality it carries. Or a correction of something you just said wrongly.

**Stage 3** — the middle two need a little pronoun stuck on the second noun,
pointing back to the first. Without that pronoun the link is not there, and
the sentence means something else.

**Stage 4** — and two of the labels describe exactly the same shape. When the
form cannot tell them apart, the honest answer names both labels rather than
inventing a difference.
