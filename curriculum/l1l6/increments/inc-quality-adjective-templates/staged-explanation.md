# Staged explanation (learner-facing)

**Stage 1** — some adjectives describe what something IS, steadily: big,
thirsty, red. Those are built on a small set of fixed shapes, and the shape
often tells you which kind of quality is meant.

**Stage 2** — each shape brings its own feminine. عطشان does not become
عطشانة; it becomes عطشى. أحمر does not take a taa either; it becomes حمراء.
The feminine is part of the shape, not an ending you attach.

**Stage 3** — two of these shapes also refuse tanwin. That is a property of
the shape, so you learn it with the shape rather than discovering it in a
sentence.

**Stage 4** — and some shapes are shared. فعيل can be a stable quality or an
intensive doer; أفعل can be a colour or a comparative. When the letters fit
two classes, our card shows both and names the question that would settle it,
instead of picking the commoner one.
