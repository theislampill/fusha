# Staged explanation (learner-facing)

**Stage 1** — Arabic can join two nouns directly to say one belongs to the
other. No linking word appears between them.

**Stage 2** — the first noun pays for the join: it drops its indefinite ending
and cannot take the article. The second noun is always genitive.

**Stage 3** — the phrase is definite or indefinite according to the LAST noun,
even though the first noun carries no article of its own. String three or more
nouns together and the same rule simply repeats.

**Stage 4** — one recognised subtype breaks the article ban on the first noun.
It is a named exception with a named analysis, not a licence to relax the
rule wherever a page looks inconvenient.
