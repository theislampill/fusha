# Staged explanation (learner-facing)

**Stage 1** — when you join two things with a connecting word, the second one
does not choose its own ending. It takes the ending the first one already
has. Two peers, one case.

**Stage 2** — that holds no matter which connector you use, and no matter
which case is involved. The connector changes the meaning: plain addition,
one thing straight after another, one thing after a gap, a choice, a
correction, a contrast.

**Stage 3** — so if the second element shows a different ending, something is
wrong with the analysis, not with the rule. Our card marks it for review and
leaves the text exactly as it was written.

**Stage 4** — and watch for look-alikes. The contrast word stops being a
connector once a pronoun attaches to it, and joining two whole sentences
gives nothing to copy. In both cases the card says the rule does not apply
here rather than forcing an answer.
