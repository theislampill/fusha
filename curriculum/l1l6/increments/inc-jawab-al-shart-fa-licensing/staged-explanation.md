# Staged explanation (learner-facing)

**Stage 1** — a conditional sentence has two halves: the condition, and the
answer to it.

**Stage 2** — sometimes the answer needs a small connective in front of it.
The test is simple: look at the FIRST word of the answer and ask whether it
can carry the ending the condition wants to give it.

**Stage 3** — if it cannot — because it is a noun, or a negator, or a future
marker, or a command — the connective goes in as a repair. If the answer is a
plain verb that CAN carry that ending, the connective is wrong, not optional.

**Stage 4** — once the connective is there, the ending on the answer's verb is
decided by whatever operator sits inside the answer, not by the condition. And
the taught list of openers is not the whole language: an answer shape outside
it gets an honest 'not covered', never a guess.
