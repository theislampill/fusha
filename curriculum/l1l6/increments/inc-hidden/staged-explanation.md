# Staged explanation (learner-facing)

**Stage 1** — Arabic often leaves out what the grammar can reconstruct:
اُكْتُبْ means "(you) write!" — the "you" is real grammar, hidden on purpose.

**Stage 2** — grammarians only reconstruct where a RULE licenses it: the
command's hidden you, the relative clause's dropped pronoun, the caller's
dropped verb in يا محمد.

**Stage 3** — where scholars reconstruct differently, we show the options —
the hidden layer is interpretation, and honest pages say whose.

**Stage 4** — the text itself never changes: hidden elements appear in the
explanation layer, never inside the words.
