# Staged explanation (learner-facing)

**Stage 1** — some nouns refuse the indefinite ending and use a shorter set of
case vowels. You spot one by a genitive slot that does not show the ending you
expect.

**Stage 2** — membership is not a feeling. It takes TWO things: the noun must
be a name or an adjective, AND it must carry one listed shape feature. One
alone is not enough — most names are perfectly ordinary.

**Stage 3** — one group joins on shape alone: certain broken plural patterns.
That is a different door into the same room.

**Stage 4** — the article, or heading a joined pair, brings the ordinary ending
back. That is plain prose grammar. Poetry can go further, but only inside
poetry, and only when you can point at the metre or rhyme that forced it.
