# Staged explanation (learner-facing)

**Stage 1** — two very different نون's can sit at the end of a present-tense
verb: تَكْتُبُونَ "you (m.pl.) write" ends in نون الرفع; يَكْتُبْنَ "they
(f.) write" ends in نون النسوة.

**Stage 2** — the tell is what comes right before the ن: نون الرفع always
follows a long vowel letter (و، ا، ي); نون النسوة attaches directly onto
the bare consonant stem, with no long vowel in between.

**Stage 3** — نون الرفع can DROP after a governor like لن/لم — but نون
النسوة is a fixed part of the word and never drops. This pack only tells
you WHICH نون a word has, never whether a sentence dropped it.
