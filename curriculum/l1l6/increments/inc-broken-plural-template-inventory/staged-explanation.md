# Staged explanation (learner-facing)

**Stage 1** — most everyday nouns do not make their plural by adding an
ending. They keep their consonants and change the vowels inside: قلم becomes
أقلام. The word is rebuilt, not extended.

**Stage 2** — the rebuilt shapes are not random. A small number of shapes
covers most of what you will read, and each one has a signature you can see:
an added opening letter, a long vowel in a fixed place, a closing pair.

**Stage 3** — but you cannot predict which shape a given word takes. Two
words of the same singular shape often take different plurals, and some words
have two plurals that are both correct. So the plural is looked up, not
computed.

**Stage 4** — and one shape is a trap: the same letters spell both a plural
and a common singular. When our card meets that skeleton it says "two
readings fit" instead of choosing. Saying so is the answer.
