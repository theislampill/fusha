# Reference — restructured (broken) plural template inventory

A restructured plural rewrites the internal vowel skeleton of its singular
instead of adding a suffix. The consonants of the root survive; the vowel
pattern does not. Case is then marked by ordinary vowels, unlike the
letter-marked suffixal plurals.

Core rows carried by this unit, each written as a shape over the supplied
radicals R1..R3 plus literal letters:

| row | skeleton | reading |
|---|---|---|
| afaal | أ R1 R2 ا R3 | plural of paucity |
| afila | أ R1 R2 R3 ة | plural of paucity |
| fuul | R1 R2 و R3 | plural of multitude |
| fiaal | R1 R2 ا R3 | plural of multitude |
| mafaail | م R1 ا R2 R3 | extreme plural |
| fualaa | R1 R2 R3 ا ء | plural of multitude |

Two facts govern every use of the table.

First, the mapping from a singular template to a plural template is
many-to-many. A singular's shape is a prior that narrows the guess; it does
not derive the plural, and a lexeme may carry more than one attested plural.
The plural is therefore looked up, never generated.

Second, one row is letter-identical to a singular template: the فعال skeleton
serves both a plural and a very common singular noun (and a third-form
masdar). The pack declares that homograph as its own row, so a surface on
that skeleton produces two survivors and the unit abstains. That abstention
is the correct answer, not a gap.

Concord is not a property of the template. A non-human plural takes feminine
singular agreement whatever row it belongs to, and a pre-posed verb is
singular regardless. Those decisions belong to the agreement units; this unit
emits none of them.
