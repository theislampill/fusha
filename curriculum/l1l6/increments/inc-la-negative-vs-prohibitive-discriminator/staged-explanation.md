# Staged explanation (learner-facing)

**Stage 1** — the same two letters can deny something and can forbid
something. "He does not write" and "do not write" begin identically.

**Stage 2** — what separates them is the ending on the verb that follows. One
leaves the verb alone; the other changes it. So you read the verb, not the
particle.

**Stage 3** — but that ending is usually a short vowel, and short vowels are
usually not written. So in ordinary text the evidence you need is simply not
there.

**Stage 4** — when that happens our card shows both readings and says the
text does not settle it. Reporting that honestly is a real answer, and it is
a better one than a confident guess.
