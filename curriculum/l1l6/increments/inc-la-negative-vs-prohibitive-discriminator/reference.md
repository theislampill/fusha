# Reference — one written shape, several functions

The shape لا covers a statement negation, a prohibitive, a categorical
negation of a whole class, a bare negative answer, and membership in frozen
expressions. The first two are the pair that matters most in reading, and
they are separated by exactly one thing.

## The verbal pair

The statement negation leaves the following imperfect verb in its default
mood: nothing has governed it. The prohibitive licenses the third mood, and
that mood is the government trace. The two differ in illocutionary force —
one denies that something happens, the other forbids it — but they are
spelled identically, so the mood exponent on the verb is the whole formal
separator.

That separator is often not in the text. For verbs whose mood is exponed by a
short vowel, unvocalised running text shows nothing at all, and the source
material teaches the mood diagnostic without ever noting this. The pack
therefore declares the unvocalised value as licit for BOTH functions. An
occurrence with that value produces two survivors and is preserved as
alternatives. That outcome is the deliverable: an annotator must be able to
report "the evidence needed to decide is not present" without either guessing
or going silent.

Where the exponent is a deleted letter rather than a vowel, the evidence
survives into unpointed text and the occurrence resolves normally.

## The other functions

The categorical negation is a different regime altogether, keyed on a
following indefinite noun bearing nasb without tanwin. It is never reached
from a verbal environment. The source lesson omits this use even though it
shares the spelling, so it is carried here as a candidate row with its own
gating.

The bare answer and the frozen expressions raise no government question at
all and are recognised from the environment rather than from the shape.

## Boundaries

The prohibitive is not restricted to the second person; well-formed
prohibitions aimed at other persons exist, so no person feature is used to
exclude it. A dependent mood that is not the third mood indicates a different
operator and is not claimed by this table. And the table has no default: a
partially specified occurrence abstains rather than leaning toward the more
familiar reading.
