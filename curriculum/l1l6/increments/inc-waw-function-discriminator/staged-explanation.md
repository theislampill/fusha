# Staged explanation (learner-facing)

**Stage 1** — the little connective is not always "and". It can join, it can
mean "along with", it can open a background clause, it can swear, and it can
simply start a new sentence.

**Stage 2** — the sentence tells you which. Look at the ending on the word
after it, look for a verb before it, and look for a pronoun tying the next
clause back to something already mentioned.

**Stage 3** — when the text is unvowelled, the ending you need may not be
there. That is not your failure; the evidence is genuinely missing.

**Stage 4** — so the page shows both readings and says why. Never translate
this word by habit.
