# Reference — connective sense discriminator

One connective carries at least five senses. Coordination joins two units that
share a case with no independent governor for the second, and is available as
a reading only where joint performance is genuinely possible. Accompaniment
requires a preceding verbal governor and an accusative following nominal that
does not share in the event. The state sense needs a definite, role-saturated
preceding nominal and a linking pronoun in the following topic-comment clause.
The oath sense stands clause-initially with a genitive nominal and a response
clause. The resumptive or parenthetical sense stands clause-initially over a
clause that fills no slot at all. Two of these separate only by the case of
the following nominal, which is unrecoverable in unpointed text; there the two
readings are preserved together.
