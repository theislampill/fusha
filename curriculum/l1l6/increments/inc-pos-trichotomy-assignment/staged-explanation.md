# Staged explanation (learner-facing)

**Stage 1** — Arabic sorts every word into three boxes: things that name,
things that happen in time, and everything left over that only means something
next to another word.

**Stage 2** — you do not guess the box, you test for it. Can it take "the"?
Can it take the doubled ending? Can it sit after a preposition? Any one yes
puts it in the naming box.

**Stage 3** — a "no" is much weaker than a "yes". A word can refuse "the" and
refuse the doubled ending and still be a naming word, because the tests are
sufficient, not required.

**Stage 4** — and when one spelling carries a naming sign and a verb sign at
once, that is two words, not one hard question. Write down both.
