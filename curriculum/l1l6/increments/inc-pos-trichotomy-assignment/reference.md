# Reference — three-way class assignment

Every word is assigned to exactly one of three classes. The naming class is
signalled by any one of five sufficient markers: the definite article,
nunation, standing after a preposition in the genitive, standing after the
vocative particle as the one called, or occupying the position about which a
comment is made. The action class is signalled by the completion particle, a
prefixed or free future marker, the vowelless feminine ending, a vowelled
subject ending, the addressee-feminine ending, the emphatic ending, or overt
person conjugation; several of these are stem-restricted and presuppose that
the perfect or imperfect stem is already identified. The residual class has no
positive marker at all: it is licensed only when both inventories fail to
attach and the word cannot stand alone as an answer. Markers are sufficient,
not necessary, so a word that rejects the article and nunation may still be of
the naming class on the predication test alone.
