# Reference — nongoverning preverbal inventory

| Function | Particle | Verb tense |
|---|---|---|
| sawfa_future_marker | سَوْفَ or سوف | imperfect |

A CLOSED registry of preverbal items that never license invented
case/mood/governor effects — this pack emits a class label only, never a
mood, case or governor key. A particle outside the closed registry
(including a genuinely GOVERNING particle like لن or لم) is never swept in;
it abstains `insufficient_features` exactly like an unrecognised surface.

**Bare قد is deliberately NOT a member of this registry.** Its two
tense-conditioned readings (confirmation-of-past before a perfect verb,
reduction-of-probability before an imperfect verb), and its secondary
recency/frequency readings, belong exclusively to
`inc-qad-tense-conditioned-sense` — the sole owner of that authority, whose
own discriminator table also preserves rivals (e.g. certainty_completion
alongside a co-surviving recency_reading) this pack must never collapse.
This pack always abstains `insufficient_features` for bare قد in either
tense rather than duplicating the owner's decision with a weaker
particle+tense-only gate.

سَوْفَ (vocalized) and سوف (ordinary unpointed) are both registered as
EXACT surface forms of the same future-marker member — no lossy
normalization collapses one into the other, and no near-miss surface
(an extra, missing or altered letter) is ever promoted from mere surface
similarity.
