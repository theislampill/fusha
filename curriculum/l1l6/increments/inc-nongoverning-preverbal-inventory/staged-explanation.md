# Staged explanation (learner-facing)

**Stage 1** — some particles sit right before a verb without changing its
case or mood at all: this pack teaches a small, CLOSED list of them —
سَوْفَ / سوف and لقد — and nothing else.

**Stage 2** — bare قد is deliberately left OUT of that list. Its reading
really does depend on the verb's tense, but that whole judgment call
belongs to a different, dedicated pack (inc-qad-tense-conditioned-sense)
that preserves both readings as rivals rather than picking one; this pack
never repeats or shortcuts that decision, so it always abstains for bare
قد in either tense.

**Stage 3** — لقد adds emphasis to a past verb, and سوف marks the future —
neither one is a mood or case rule, just a meaning shade, and this pack
never emits a mood, case, or governor key for either.

**Stage 4** — this is a short, closed list: a real GOVERNING particle like
لن or لم (which does change the verb's mood) is deliberately kept OUT, so
it never gets mistaken for one of these gentle, nongoverning ones.
