# Staged explanation (learner-facing)

**Stage 1** — Arabic shrinks a word with one small shape: insert a ي right
after the second root letter and add short vowels — كَلْب "dog" →
كُلَيْب "little dog/puppy".

**Stage 2** — that ي is part of the SHAPE, not a root letter: رجل ("man")
becomes رُجَيْل, and the extra ي never gets counted as one of the three root
consonants.

**Stage 3** — matching the shape alone isn't proof: our hover only commits
once it also knows the base word is a real, attested word AND that the ي
really is the diminutive marker (not a coincidence of spelling).

**Stage 4** — a hollow base (one whose middle root letter is a weak و/ي,
like بَاب "door") keeps that weak letter written out literally in the
diminutive (بُوَيْب) — but only where our pack has explicitly said that
restoration is licensed; otherwise we say "needs review" instead of
guessing.
