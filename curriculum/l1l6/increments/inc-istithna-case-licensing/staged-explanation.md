# Staged explanation (learner-facing)

**Stage 1** — an exclusion sentence names a group, then pulls one member back
out of it. Three pieces: the group, the exclusion word, the excluded item.

**Stage 2** — the case on the excluded item is not decided by the exclusion
word. Two questions decide it: is the sentence a statement or a negation, and
is the group actually named?

**Stage 3** — when the group is not named, the excluded item is simply doing
the clause's own job, so the clause gives it its case — it can end up
nominative, accusative or genitive.

**Stage 4** — under negation with the group named, the tradition licenses two
readings at once. An honest page shows both instead of silently picking one.
