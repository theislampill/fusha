# Staged explanation (learner-facing)

**Stage 1** — Arabic negatives are little machines, not just "not": each
one picks a tense and a verb form.

**Stage 2** — لم flips time: the verb LOOKS present, the meaning is past
(لم يذهبْ = he did not go).

**Stage 3** — لن locks the future (لن أذهبَ = I will never go); لا with a
"you"-verb is a command not to.

**Stage 4** — bare لا before a noun can even deny a whole category
(لا إلهَ إلا الله). When the sentence doesn't show enough, we say "needs
context" instead of guessing.
