# Staged explanation (learner-facing)

**Stage 1** — mood is not one ending. It is a table: what the verb shows
depends on what kind of verb it is.

**Stage 2** — a plain strong verb swaps its last vowel. A verb carrying a final
nun drops that nun. A verb ending in a weak letter loses the letter. A hollow
verb loses the letter in its middle.

**Stage 3** — several of these signals are things that are NOT there. An
absence is evidence, and an empty final position does not mean "no mood".

**Stage 4** — and for one whole class, two different moods look exactly alike.
There you must read the governing particle, because the verb itself has
stopped telling you.
