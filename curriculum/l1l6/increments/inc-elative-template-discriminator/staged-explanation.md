# Staged explanation (learner-facing)

**Stage 1** — one shape in Arabic can be a comparison word, a colour word, or
a verb. Same letters, three different jobs. So the first question is never
"what does it mean" but "what kind of word is it here".

**Stage 2** — for the comparison word, the shape does not tell you whether it
means "bigger" or "biggest". The sentence does: a following "than" phrase
gives you one, the definite article or a following noun gives you the other.

**Stage 3** — colour and defect words wear the same shape. What separates
them is their feminine partner, and that is something you look up, not
something you can see in the letters.

**Stage 4** — when the evidence really does fit two readings, our card shows
both instead of picking. Nothing about this shape has a default, so a guess
would not be a small error — it would put the word in the wrong class.
