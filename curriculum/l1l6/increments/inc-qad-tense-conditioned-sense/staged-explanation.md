# Staged explanation (learner-facing)

**Stage 1** — this little particle does not have one meaning. It has a meaning
per neighbour.

**Stage 2** — put a completed verb after it and it says "certainly, already".
Put an ongoing verb after it and it says "possibly, sometimes". Those are
opposite strengths from the same two letters.

**Stage 3** — context can add a second layer: "has just now" on one side,
"often does" on the other. These sit next to the first reading, not on top of
it.

**Stage 4** — and note what it never does: it changes no ending on the verb.
A particle can carry meaning without carrying government.
