# Staged explanation (learner-facing)

**Stage 1** — a noun from the reduced-ending class does not always show its
reduced ending. Two situations bring the ordinary case vowel back.

**Stage 2** — both are the same situation underneath: the article, and heading
a joined pair, each make the noun definite.

**Stage 3** — the vowel comes back; the indefinite ending does NOT. It marks
indefiniteness, and the noun is now definite. Two separate questions, two
separate answers.

**Stage 4** — only the term that actually became definite is affected. Sitting
as the SECOND noun of a joined pair changes nothing. And poetry may show what
prose forbids — check the register before calling a form an error.
