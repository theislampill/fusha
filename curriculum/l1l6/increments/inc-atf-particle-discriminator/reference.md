# Reference — coordination particle discriminator

The coordinator set is closed and its formal content is case transmission: a
particle standing between two units that share one case, with no independent
governor for the second. Within the set, the three additive particles differ
by sequencing, the two alternation particles split by clause type in the
classical division, correction differs from adversative contrast by whether
the first term is cancelled or upgraded, one member negates the second term
instead of adding it, one member includes an extreme, and one member is paired
and requires its partner to close the alternation. Three forms in the set are
homographic with non-coordinating items: the negator, whose coordinating use
stands between two single terms while clause negation precedes a predicate;
the adversative, whose geminated counterpart belongs to a governing family;
and the extreme-inclusion member, whose coordinator status is itself disputed
and is therefore kept as attributed alternatives.
