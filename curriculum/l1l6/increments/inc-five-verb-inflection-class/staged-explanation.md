# Staged explanation (learner-facing)

**Stage 1** — five present-tense verb shapes ("الأفعال الخمسة") are built by
adding a long vowel letter (و، ا، or ي) plus ن right after the stem:
تَكْتُبُونَ "you (m. pl.) write", يَكْتُبُونَ "they (m.) write".

**Stage 2** — the five shapes are told apart just by their letters: the
prefix (ت for "you", ي for "he/they") and the ending (ون، ين، ان) never
overlap with each other.

**Stage 3** — this hover only tells you WHICH of the five shapes a word has
— never whether the sentence needs its ن dropped (that happens after لن/لم
and is a separate grammar fact, not a shape fact).

**Stage 4** — a look-alike ending that has NO long vowel before its ن
(تَكْتُبْنَ) is a completely different family — the "نون النسوة" — and this
pack never confuses the two.
