# Staged explanation (learner-facing)

**Stage 1** — a describing word must match the noun it describes on four
things at once: case, gender, number, and whether it is definite.

**Stage 2** — one override comes first. If the noun is plural and its
referents are not people, the describing word goes feminine singular. That is
matching, not breaking the rule.

**Stage 3** — definiteness is the axis that changes what the phrase IS. A
definite noun with an indefinite describing word after it stops being a phrase
and becomes a whole statement.

**Stage 4** — after a chain of joined nouns, the case on the describing word
tells you which noun it belongs to. When both nouns share that case, the
honest answer is that the ending cannot tell you — and the page should say
so.
