# Staged explanation (learner-facing)

**Stage 1** — most letters can send a connecting stroke out to the letter
that follows them. Six letters never do, no matter what comes next.

**Stage 2** — whether a letter also RECEIVES a connection from before depends
on that earlier letter: if nothing precedes it, or if the letter before it is
itself one of the six, there is nothing coming in.

**Stage 3** — put those two answers together and you get the shape: neither
connection is standalone, incoming-only is final, outgoing-only is initial,
both is medial. The six non-attaching letters can only ever be standalone or
final.

**Stage 4** — a few letters look like members of the six-letter set but are
not covered by this table, and a hamza sitting on a letter other than alif is
not automatically treated as that letter either. When the table does not
recognise the letter, it says so instead of guessing from the resemblance.
