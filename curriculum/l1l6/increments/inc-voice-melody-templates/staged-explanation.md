# Staged explanation (learner-facing)

**Stage 1** — Arabic does not add a word to make a verb passive. It changes
the vowels inside the verb and leaves every consonant where it was.

**Stage 2** — so كتب and كتب are the same three letters twice: one says "he
wrote", the other says "it was written". The difference is real, and it is
entirely in the vowels.

**Stage 3** — which means that in ordinary unvowelled text, the voice of most
verbs is simply not visible. Our card does not pick the commoner reading. It
shows both and says the letters do not decide. That is the true answer.

**Stage 4** — a few cells escape. When the middle letter of the root is weak,
the passive spells itself differently: قال against قيل. There the letters do
decide, and the card says so. Learn which cells you can trust.
