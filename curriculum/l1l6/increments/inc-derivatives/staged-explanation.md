# Staged explanation (learner-facing)

**Stage 1** — Arabic builds "doer", "done-to", "doing", and "place-of" words
with fixed shapes: كَاتِب writer، مَكْتُوب written، مَكْتَب office — one root
ك ت ب, three shapes.

**Stage 2** — derived forms use مُـ for both doer and done-to; the small
vowel before the last letter decides: مُعَلِّم (i = teacher) vs مُعَلَّم
(a = taught).

**Stage 3** — no vowel written? Then we honestly can't tell from the letters
alone — the reader (and our hover) says "needs the vowel" instead of
guessing.

**Stage 4** — these are all NOUN shapes. Even though they come from verbs,
their meaning is a person/thing/event, so their gloss is nominal.
