# Staged explanation (learner-facing)

**Stage 1** — the article is spelled the same every time. What changes is
whether you pronounce its laam.

**Stage 2** — the letter right after the article belongs to one of two fixed
lists, fourteen and fourteen. If it is on the moon list, the laam is always
pronounced and nothing doubles — no need to check anything else.

**Stage 3** — if it is on the sun list, that only means assimilation is
POSSIBLE. To actually claim it happened here, you need to see the doubling
mark written on that letter. No mark, no claim — even if you are confident
the word is one you know.

**Stage 4** — whichever way it goes, the article's own laam is still the
article's laam. Doubling a letter after the article never means that letter's
root is doubled, and it never turns the word into a Form II shape.
