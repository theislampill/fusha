## Exact nonclaims

- The projection hash proves byte-stable equality of the selected `segments`, `glosses`, `morphline`, `root`, and `facts` payload; it does not prove that any morphology, syntax, root, gloss, or fact is linguistically correct.
- `appearance_count` counts appearances represented by the supplied whitelist metadata and conservative entry-store reconciliation; it is not a browser impression, DOM-node, page-load, or learner-interaction count.
- The entry store currently supplies ayah-only refs: no exact word-level loc refs were present. The 3,318 unresolved refs are not silently assigned to words, and the report does not claim complete word-level entry attribution.
- Zero `emphasis` fields in this artifact does not prove that no runtime page visually emphasizes a word; the supplied source did not provide a field used for that claim.
- The index does not claim complete production entry-page coverage beyond the two supplied source surfaces, and it does not establish that every public/runtime copy has been captured.
- Same normalized surfaces are deliberately not merged across canonical locations; the `39:63:3`/`22:18:9` pair is evidence of that boundary, not a parity failure.
- This lane made no change to either sibling data input, no live-site mutation, no push, and no publication or release claim.
