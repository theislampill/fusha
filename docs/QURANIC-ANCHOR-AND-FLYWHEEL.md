# The Qurʾānic Anchor and the Flywheel — project charter

**Status:** first-class project charter (owner-adopted 2026-07-10). This document governs
how the project describes itself. Companion: `docs/QURANIC-ANCHOR-EVALUATION-PLAN.md`
(the falsifiable comparative program). Every substantial claim in this document — and in
any project document that cites it — carries one of four explicit epistemic labels:

```
ARCHITECTURAL COMMITMENT       an accepted design direction of the project
OBSERVED RESULT                demonstrated by current artifacts or tests
TESTABLE HYPOTHESIS            a falsifiable prediction awaiting the comparative program
OWNER/THEOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION   the owner's interpretive position, preserved as such
```

These levels must not be blurred. Moving a claim upward (hypothesis → observed) requires
the evidence the evaluation plan defines; moving it downward requires an owner decision.
The distinction is not a hedge: **the project is building the instrument that can make the
stronger claims testable.**

## 1. The governing thesis

Transclusion gives every lexical, morphological, syntactic, rhetorical, and pedagogical
analysis a stable, source-addressed home. The meta-transclusive ṣarf/naḥw fact lattice
preserves compatible and competing analyses around that home instead of prematurely
collapsing them. Qamus review improves the lattice; the lattice accelerates subsequent
Qamus review; and the resulting structured alternatives are intended to power both
arbitrary-text analysis and explanation-rich tutoring.

The Qurʾān is the project's privileged anchor because it joins a closed and canonically
addressed text, an explicitly transmitted and vocalized reading, exceptional internal
patterning, continuing pedagogical centrality, and an unparalleled density of inherited
and modern lexical, morphological, syntactic, recitational, rhetorical, exegetical, and
legal-linguistic analysis.

Classical Arabic supplies an unusually structured ambiguity space through roots, patterns,
derivational families, clitics, inflection, government, case, mood, agreement, and
context. The Qurʾān supplies a fixed and densely analysed realization of that system. The
project's central thesis is that this conjunction makes the Qurʾān an exceptionally
powerful — and potentially unmatched — seed corpus for a provenance-preserving,
ambiguity-aware fluency engine.

## 2. The flywheel — ARCHITECTURAL COMMITMENT

```
canonically addressed Qurʾānic occurrences
→ provenance-tracked lexical and grammatical facts
→ competing and compatible ṣarf/naḥw analyses
→ reusable candidate-generation and proof rules
→ faster later Qurʾānic review
→ increasingly capable arbitrary-text analysis
→ controlled expansion into later Fusha corpora
→ new examples, exceptions, and rules flowing back into the engine
```

The Nawawī Forty, the Ṣaḥīḥayn, and later corpora are extensions of the coordinate
system, not replacements for the Qurʾānic anchor. Later corpora contribute vocabulary,
constructions, and stylistic range, but they enter an already stabilised grammatical
coordinate system, retaining traceability, comparability, and ambiguity.

Why a lattice and not a parse: because short vowels are ordinarily omitted, several
morphologically valid readings may initially occupy the same written form. This ambiguity
is not formless noise — it is generated by a comparatively constrained system of roots,
templates, particles, inflections, and grammatical relations. The language naturally
produces a finite candidate space:

```
surface token
→ possible clitic segmentations
→ possible roots and patterns
→ possible inflectional analyses
→ possible syntactic governors and roles
→ contextually scored parse alternatives
```

The lattice is a computational representation of structure already present within the
language. A single-parse system prematurely collapses that structure; a lattice retains
it until sufficient evidence permits a principled selection.

## 3. ARCHITECTURAL COMMITMENTS (accepted project directions)

- source-addressed transclusion;
- preservation of competing ṣarf and naḥw readings;
- typed fact lattices rather than premature single-parse collapse;
- contextual scoring and certification (two-vote and stronger gates; fail-closed defaults);
- the Qamus/lattice flywheel;
- later-corpus expansion from a Qurʾānic anchor;
- arbitrary-text parsing and tutoring as downstream consumers;
- provenance and ambiguity are never traded away for throughput.

## 5. TESTABLE HYPOTHESES (the comparative research program — strong, explicit, falsifiable)

The owner's thesis predicts, and the evaluation plan operationalizes (H-numbers refer to
`docs/QURANIC-ANCHOR-EVALUATION-PLAN.md`):

- **H1** — the Qurʾān produces greater provenance completeness (canonical addressing,
  token-identity and version stability, vocalization coverage) than alternative seed
  corpora;
- **H2** — it carries a higher inherited-analysis density per token and construction;
- **H3** — it produces higher safe-transclusion and projection yield and lower
  unresolved-exception rates, and accelerates later review more strongly (flywheel yield);
- **H4** — its structured repetitions provide superior controlled comparisons, and
  lattice-preserving analysis outperforms early-single-parse collapse on it;
- **H5** — a Qurʾān-seeded engine transfers efficiently to the Nawawī Forty, the
  Ṣaḥīḥayn, and other Classical Arabic corpora, and competing analyses remain more
  traceable and stable when grounded first in the Qurʾānic coordinate system;
- **H6** — the conjunction of canonical fixity, vocalization, addressing, annotation
  density, and internal patterning is unmatched at comparable depth **after controlling
  for corpus size, annotation effort, reviewer expertise, and evaluation budget** — the
  advantage is intrinsic to the conjunction, not merely to the volume of historical work;
- **H7** — Qurʾānic composition yields a lattice-level literary/rhetorical signature that
  resists reduction to the recognised categories (the sixteen poetic metres, sajʿ,
  ordinary prose) in ways representative Fusha corpora do not — a hypothesis that REQUIRES
  the additional prosodic/rhetorical representation layer before it can be evaluated at
  all (see plan §H7; the current ṣarf/naḥw lattice alone cannot establish it).

These are not aspirations; they are predictions with measurable outcomes and defined
failure conditions. If controlled comparison shows another corpus sustaining the same
architecture at equal fidelity under equal budgets, H6 is falsified as stated and the
charter must record that result.

## 6. OWNER/THEOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION (preserved; not an engineering claim)

The owner's interpretive position, recorded verbatim in intent:

If controlled comparative evaluation shows that the Qurʾān uniquely or exceptionally
sustains high-fidelity provenance, ambiguity preservation, projection, review
acceleration, cross-corpus transfer, and explanatory generation, the owner regards that
evidence as computationally consonant with the classical Islamic claims of the Qurʾān's
unique excellence and inimitability (iʿjāz).

The owner likewise understands the Prophet ﷺ — his lack of literary schooling, the
Qurʾān's historical reception among the master rhetoricians of its milieu, and the
recorded inability of human composition to match it — as part of the same theological
phenomenon to which such computational evidence would speak. The historical observation
that the best rhetoricians could not place the Qurʾān within any known literary category
("neither one nor the other") is, in this reading, what H7 would operationalize.

**Boundary (binding on all engineering documents):** the current 1047-test harness, the
current compiler, and the current Qamus corpus have NOT proved — and must not be described
as having proved — divine origin; theological perfection; the absence of every possible
contradiction; literary inimitability; that no human text can ever reproduce the measured
properties; or that all other corpora necessarily fail. Those conclusions require either
theological reasoning outside the engineering system or future comparative evidence. The
project may generate evidence relevant to them without pretending a software regression
suite deductively establishes them.

## 7. Reading- and edition-specific provenance

"Stable vocalization" is a precise, edition-scoped statement. Every Qurʾānic source
identity used by the lattice should ultimately be able to state:

```
qirāʾa · riwāya · rasm/orthographic edition · digital text edition ·
edition/source hash · sūrah · āyah · token identity
```

The current system is anchored to its selected Tanzil/Uthmānī representation and the
transmitted reading it encodes. Project documents must not imply that all canonical
readings (qirāʾāt) have identical token surfaces or vocalization. Future qirāʾāt support
enters as explicit source variants or branches — never as corruption of the one canonical
identity currently anchored. (Future schema/research requirement; no product schema is
expanded solely for this in T6–T8.)

## 8. Language discipline (present-tense engineering claims)

Until the comparative evaluation exists, engineering documents do not state as observed:
"the system works cleanly only on the Qurʾān"; "every alternative starting text breaks the
flywheel"; "no other corpus can sustain the architecture"; "the code proves
inimitability"; "the lattice proves perfection or lack of error"; "all ordinary Fusha maps
into poetry, sajʿ, or prose while the Qurʾān does not"; "the Qurʾān's structure cannot be
replicated by human composition."

They say instead: "the project is designed to test…"; "the owner's thesis is…"; "current
evidence is consistent with…"; "the comparative hypothesis predicts…"; "the result would
provide computational support for…"; "not yet evaluated against controlled alternative
corpora."

The stronger claims are not deleted — they live, at full strength, in §5 and §6 of this
charter and in the evaluation plan, where they belong until the instrument this project is
building has measured them.
