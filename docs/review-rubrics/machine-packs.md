# Machine-pack authoring and review rubric

Read before authoring each coherent pack family and during train review.

- Draft from qualified unit tables, but remove every `draft` marker before a pack can validate.
- Nothing is licensed by default: scope rules to the exact template, slot, radical class and written evidence they justify.
- Separate root, pattern/augment, clitic, inflection, fused orthography and hover-only diacritic information.
- Preserve weak-root, hamza, gemination, substitution and contraction guards; do not globalize a local realization licence.
- Require exact surface reconstruction and hostile non-word fixtures, including literal and substituted weak realizations.
- Treat unpointed or missing evidence as no evidence, never as confirmation.
- Preserve rivals and abstention; pack output is candidate machinery and cannot certify an occurrence.
- Prove the real directory-discovered consumer loads the pack and its fixtures.
- Emit binding/crosswalk/accounting rows with the same batch; no consumer-less candidate artifact.
- Compose repository paths from POSIX-style relative strings with `pathlib.Path`; never rewrite `/` to a Windows-only separator. Exercise every new focused test through the Linux canonical harness.
- Derive packet inventories, bank counts and row denominators from the same effective registry loader used at runtime. A code-injected bank must not be invisible to a raw-contract-only gate.
- Mark zero-row closure dimensions as vacuously satisfied and count closed
  units that rely on them. Independently rederive closure from the dimensions
  in the validator rather than comparing two outputs from one builder pass.
- Run focused family fixtures per batch and amortize the full harness/review at the assembled slice.

Record findings by stable class so recurrence can be measured across tranches.

## Feedforward checklist (from the T2 template-classification round)
- Complete seven-part pack contract (unit, fixtures, reference, procedure, staged explanation, hover
  fields, guards) before freeze — never freeze a pack on unit+fixtures alone.
- The validator must not discover missing support files after review; run
  `tools/validate_curriculum_l1l6.py` before and after a batch and require zero missing-file failures
  for every pack the batch touches.
- Eval coverage claims require observed production-consumer calls, not structural registration: a bank
  may claim `implemented_and_consumed` only once its adapter genuinely invokes the declared consumer for
  every row and a mutation/hostile proof shows rebinding or bypassing that consumer turns the bank red —
  an eval fixture's own expected answer can never certify a fact by itself.
